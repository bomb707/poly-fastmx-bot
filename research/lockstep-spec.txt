===============================================================================
Lockstep — STRATEGY SPECIFICATION  (BTC 5-minute "Up or Down")
===============================================================================
The strategy described here replaced an earlier experimental signal-hedge core on
2026-07-22). Code: engine/strategy.js (stepSignalHedge) + engine/intensity.js
(volatility sensor). Backtest driver: research/lockstep-backtest.mjs.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. MARKET
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Polymarket "BTC Up or Down" — a fresh binary market every 5 minutes. OPEN price
recorded at the start, CLOSE at the end. CLOSE>OPEN ⇒ "Up" pays $1/share; else
"Down" pays $1. Losing side pays $0. Shares trade $0–$1 (≈ implied probability).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. CORE IDEA
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
As a round runs down, less time remains for BTC to move. If BTC has already
moved further from the open than it could realistically move back in the time
left, the current leader is very likely the final winner even though the market
hasn't settled. Lockstep detects that, BUYS THE WINNING side (the "lock"), then
buys the near-dead LOSING side for pennies (the "hedge") — a paired $1 set that
pays out no matter how the round settles, for a combined cost below $1.
Signal is pure price + recent realized volatility.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. THE TWO NUMBERS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
GAP           = current BTC spot − round OPEN ($).  Its SIGN is the current
                leader (Up if >0). Source: tk.bzGap (Binance spot − window open).

POSSIBLE MOVE = INTENSITY × timeLeftFraction(secsRemaining)  + edge buffer
  INTENSITY   = max (or trimmed-mean "smooth") of the last L_VOL_ROUNDS (6 ≈
                30 min) COMPLETED rounds' EXCURSIONS. A round's excursion =
                max |price − open| during the round. The ACTIVE round is
                EXCLUDED (else the estimate rises to meet the live move and the
                lock never fires).
  timeLeftFrac= secsRemaining / 300 (linear) OR √(secsRemaining/300) (sqrt).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. ENTRY (lock)      |GAP| > POSSIBLE MOVE   → buy the leader at the ask
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Gates: intensity ready (≥6 rounds); winner ask ≥ L_ENTRY_FLOOR (0.50 — below
that the market itself sees a coin flip); optional ceiling; no new locks in the
final L_SKIP_END_S (5s) or first L_SKIP_OPEN_S (0); ≥ L_MIN_TIME_S left; one
lock per round (L_ONE_PER_ROUND); ≤ L_MAX_ENTRIES buys/round; L_COOLDOWN_MS
between buys; optional UTC pause windows; optional L_MIN_GAP / L_EDGE_BUFFER.
Default lock size = SIZE (40 shares).

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. HEDGE (grab the cheap loser → complete a $1 set)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
PRICE mode (default): buy the loser when its ask ≤ L_HEDGE_CAP (0.02) AND the
completed pair still nets > 0 after fees.
PROFIT mode: buy when the completed pair nets ≥ L_HEDGE_MIN (0.03) after fees.
Hedge size matches the leg (balanced set). If the loser never gets cheap enough,
the bot does NOT force a hedge — it rides the (likely) winner to settlement.
L_END_HEDGE_S (0 = off) can force a late hedge, but the backtest shows that
LOSES (buying the loser expensive near close) — keep it off.

-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. COSTS
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Polymarket crypto-tier taker fee = 7% × px·(1−px) × shares (largest mid-priced,
tiny near $0/$1). Both legs trade at price extremes ⇒ low-fee zones. Fees are
folded into every profit check (engine/strategy.js fillFee).

===============================================================================
7. BACKTEST FINDINGS  (btc 5m, backtest API v-default, taker fees, causal
   cross-round intensity; research/lockstep-backtest.mjs)
===============================================================================
Latency matters enormously — Lockstep locks in the final seconds, where the ask
moves fastest. The active replay invariant is maker 130 ms decision-to-queue and
taker 520 ms decision-to-fill. Results per config, NET after fees:

                              7 days           30 days
  linear (as first specced)  −$565             −$2818   ← LATENCY-DEAD
  √ scaling only             +$83             +$14     ← break-even
  √ + edge buffer $8         +$343  ($0.37/f) +$596 ($0.15/f, WR 96.5%) ← BEST
  √ + forced end-hedge       −$400            −$1849   ← counterproductive

WHY: a random walk's range grows as √time, so LINEAR scaling under-estimates the
possible move mid-round and locks too many undecided rounds → latency-dead.
√ scaling + a ~$8 edge buffer locks only GENUINELY-decided rounds.

COMPOSITION (the important caveat): the profit is the ARB (hedged $1-sets,
+$4083/30d at eb8). The unhedged "naked winner" legs LOSE money (−$3487/30d)
even at 90% win-rate — the 10% reversals cost ~$36 each. Net (+$596) is the
small difference of two large opposing flows ⇒ THIN and FRAGILE: worse real
latency, adverse selection on the 2¢ hedge fill, or a fee change can flip it
negative. This is a modeled backtest — NOT yet validated on real fills.

DEFAULTS set to the only viable config: L_SCALING="sqrt", L_EDGE_BUFFER=8
(⚠ the buffer is in $ of the underlying — re-tune per asset).
